Many of us are tired in a way sleep doesn't fix. The to-do list never ends, the phone is always on, and even our days off feel like catching up.

Into that tiredness, Jesus speaks a simple invitation: "Come to Me, all you who labor and are heavy laden, and I will give you rest."

## Rest is a gift, not a reward

We tend to treat rest as something we earn once everything is finished. But everything is never finished. God built rest into creation before anyone had done a day's work, and He commanded a day of rest for people who still had fields to plough.

Rest says: the world does not depend on me. I can stop, because God doesn't.

## Practising rest

- **Set a boundary.** Choose a few hours each week with no work and no screens, and protect them.
- **Do something that fills you.** A walk, a meal with friends, music, reading for pleasure.
- **Rest your mind as well as your body.** Hand your worries to God in prayer before you stop, and leave them with Him.
- **Rest together.** Some of the most restful hours are spent with people we love.

## Trusting God with what's left undone

The hardest part of rest is leaving things unfinished. But that is exactly where the trust is. Every time we stop, we practise believing that God is God and we are not.

This week, take Jesus at His word. Come to Him, and rest.
