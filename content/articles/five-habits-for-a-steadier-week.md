Meetings are good. But most of the Christian life happens in between them: on the commute, at the kitchen table, in the ten minutes before sleep. Here are five small habits that help keep the week steady.

## 1. Start with the Word, not the phone

Before the messages and the news, read a few verses. It doesn't need to be a long passage. A single psalm or a few verses from a Gospel is enough to set the direction of the day.

## 2. Pray on a schedule, and off it

Pick a regular time to pray, and keep it. Then add the unscheduled prayers: a sentence at a red light, a thank-you while washing up. Both kinds matter.

## 3. Carry one verse

Write one verse on a card or your phone's lock screen and come back to it through the day. By Thursday you will know it by heart, and it will have been working on you all week.

## 4. Check on someone

Send one message a day to someone in the fellowship: "Praying for you today." It takes ten seconds and it changes how connected we feel.

## 5. End the day with gratitude

Before you sleep, name three things you are thankful for. It trains the eyes to see God's kindness in ordinary days.

> These habits won't make you holy on their own. But they put you, again and again, in the places where grace tends to find people.

None of this is complicated, and that is the point. Faithfulness is mostly made of small things done often.
