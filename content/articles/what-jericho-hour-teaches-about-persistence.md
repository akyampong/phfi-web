Once a month, on the last Wednesday, we stay up late to pray. We call it Jericho Hour, and the name is deliberate.

## A strange battle plan

When Israel reached Jericho, God didn't give them siege engines. He told them to walk around the city once a day for six days, and seven times on the seventh, and then to shout. For six days nothing happened. The walls stood exactly where they had been.

Imagine the third day. Or the fifth. Walking in silence around a wall that hadn't so much as cracked.

## Persistence is faith in motion

The walls didn't fall because Israel walked well. They fell because God had promised. But Israel's part was to keep walking until the promise was fulfilled.

Prayer is often like that. We pray for a family member for years. We bring the same need month after month. Jesus told a parable about a widow who kept coming back to an unjust judge, "that men always ought to pray and not lose heart."

## Why we pray together

Persistence is hard alone. That is one reason we gather: to carry each other's walls. When your faith is tired, someone else's prayer keeps walking for you.

If there's a wall in your life that hasn't moved, bring it to Jericho Hour. Walk around it with us one more time.
