Forgiveness is one of the most beautiful words in the Christian vocabulary, and one of the hardest to live out. It is easy to talk about in general. It is much harder with a name and a face attached.

## What forgiveness isn't

It helps to clear away some misunderstandings:

- **It isn't saying it was fine.** Wrong is wrong. Forgiveness takes the hurt seriously.
- **It isn't forgetting.** You may remember for a long time. Forgiveness is about what you do with the memory.
- **It isn't always reconciliation.** Trust may need to be rebuilt slowly, and sometimes boundaries are wise.

## What it is

Paul writes, "be kind to one another, tenderhearted, forgiving one another, even as God in Christ forgave you." That last phrase is the key. We forgive because we have been forgiven, at great cost, by Someone we had wronged.

Forgiveness is releasing the debt. It is deciding not to make them pay, not to keep the account open, not to rehearse the case against them every night.

## When the feelings don't follow

Often the decision comes first and the feelings come later. You may have to forgive the same thing many times, each time the memory returns. That isn't failure; it is what forgiveness looks like in real life.

> Forgiveness is a decision you make once and then keep making.

If there's someone you are struggling to forgive, start by praying for them, honestly. Ask God to help you want to forgive. And if you need someone to pray with you, we would be glad to.
